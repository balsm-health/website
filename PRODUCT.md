# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Arab patients (Alexandria to Aswan — urban and underserved equally) navigating fragmented healthcare with no continuous record, no data visibility, no way to connect pharmacy, clinic, and lab into one picture. Healthcare providers — pharmacists, doctors, nurses, lab operators — who need a system they own and trust. Clinic/hospital operations managers who justify infrastructure decisions and care about self-hosting, PDPL compliance, and zero licensing cost.

## Product Purpose

Balsm is the Community-Owned Healthcare OS for the Arab world — free to self-host, Arabic-first, offline-ready, FHIR-native. The website is its public face: a coming-soon landing page that communicates the mission, earns trust, and captures early-access interest from providers and patients who've never had software built for them.

## Brand Personality

Trusted professional. Serious when it counts (clinical contexts, compliance, data handling). Optimistic where it matters (community, mission, open-source). Never hyped, preachy, corporate, or apologetic.

Three words: Calm. Confident. Ours.

## Anti-references

- SaaS startup gradient-bubble aesthetic (blob backgrounds + glassmorphism cards = consumer, not clinical)
- Teal-dominant palette (previous website mistake — teal is a petal color, not the primary action color)
- Warm-cream AI default body background (cream is for document surfaces, not the page)
- Generic medical stock photography / cross / syringe brand symbols
- Corporate "healthcare software" language
- Cool blue-gray (Tailwind slate) neutral scale — brand uses warm olive-gray (ink)

## Design Principles

1. **Arabic is first-class** — بَلسَم with full diacritics, RTL native, IBM Plex Sans Arabic + Cairo for display, never an afterthought.
2. **Clinical restraint** — calm, not flashy. Healthcare deserves stillness. No bounce, no glass cards, no eyebrow on every section.
3. **Five petals, one brand** — the flower mark is the identity. Blue (#1283FF) is the primary action. Aqua is the healing accent. All five petals appear in brand moments.
4. **Warmth through ink, not background** — warm olive-gray neutral scale (not cool slate), cream for document surfaces only. The page reads warm through typography and color, not a tinted body background.
5. **Sovereignty visible** — every touchpoint reinforces: "your data, your system." Calm confidence, earned trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. RTL-native (Arabic and English first-class). Reduced motion alternatives for all animations. Arabic numerals and Egyptian date/currency formatting for ar locale. Offline-awareness in messaging (the product itself is offline-first).
